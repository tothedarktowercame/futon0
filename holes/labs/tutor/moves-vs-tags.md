# Moves versus star-map tags

The cascades below follow the order in each worked lesson. A tag is listed only
when it points at some move in that cascade, rather than merely occurring on the
problem's star-map row.

ordered decisions, applicability signals, and reasons that the constant tags
omit. The strongest repeated evidence is the count/connectedness move in
`a96A07` and `t98J02`; every other claimed move family currently rests on one
lesson, and even the overall comparison has only these three examples.
