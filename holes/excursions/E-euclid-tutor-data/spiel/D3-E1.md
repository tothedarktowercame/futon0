# DESIGN-3 E1: ZERO hindsight-goal yield

Date: 2026-11-12.  Label: **ZERO**.  No Book proof, prefix, goal, or policy
target was used.  Each sample began with only the givens of I.1, I.2, or I.3
and chose six moves uniformly from the engine's complete guarded legal list.

## Configuration and boundary

- 12 processes, `PYTHONHASHSEED=0`, one BLAS/OpenMP thread per process.
- Seed `2026111201`; 30-minute sampling deadline.
- Own `futon-testing.slice` scope, `MemoryMax=24G`, `TasksMax=1024`.
- Output: `spiel/loop-smoke/d3-e1/` (ignored).
- 97 six-move runs emitted records.  There were 57,303 records before
  deduplication.

The first measurement exposed a metric enumerator bug and is excluded.  LA
rows contain `(atom, coefficient)` pairs; the first implementation treated
the pair as an atom, yielding no metric goals.  Commit `766fad5` fixes that
and adds an I.1 regression.  In the corrected run, nine workers stopped
naturally; three were still minimizing their last pre-deadline runs 5m36s
later and were stopped by their recorded job and scope.  All already-streamed
records are retained.  Commit `2205289` makes the deadline interrupt goal
enumeration, so later runs do not have this drain.  No worker or scope
remained afterward.

## Goal set and canonicalization

Non-metric goals are new `State.facts` mentioning a constructed object.
Their needed sets follow the recorded `(rule, premise facts)` provenance,
including creators of objects in the chain, and are replay-minimized.
Metric goals are exact entailed `=`, `<`, or `>` comparisons among distinct
live segment atoms and exact `=`, `<`, or `>` comparisons among distinct live
angle atoms, provided a constructed object occurs.  For `L` live lengths and
`A` live angles the per-position bound is
`3*(choose(L,2)+choose(A,2))`.  Metric needed sets use deletion replay.

The canonical key replaces constructed names by `made0`, `made1`, ... in
needed-move creation order, then serializes the goal and needed rule/argument/
binding tuples.  A pair is `(proposition givens, canonical key)`.

spanning all three givens and needed sizes 2--5.  Each row replayed only its
recorded needed indices from a fresh start and asked the engine whether the

## Five examples

The notation is the engine/CLI rule notation.  `|ab|` denotes segment length.

1. I.1: `extend_point_longer(AB,a,b,b,a) -> x_2`;
   `extend_point_longer(AB,b,a,x_2,b) -> x_3`.  Goal: `|ab| < |a x_3|`.
2. I.2: `extend_point_longer(BC,c,b,a_1,a_1) -> x_2`;
   `point_between_points_shorter_than(BC,x_2,b,b,x_2) -> a_3`.
   Goal: `|a_3 c| > |bc|`.
3. I.2: the same two moves as example 2.  Goal: `between(c,b,a_3)`.
4. I.3: `circle_from_points(b,a) -> C_1`;
   `intersection_circle_line_extending_points(C_1,AB,b,a) -> g_6`.
   Goal: `|ab| = |b g_6|`.
5. I.3: `extend_point_longer(C,c1,c0,c0,c1) -> x_1`;
   `extend_point_longer(C,x_1,c0,a,b) -> x_2`.  Goal: `on_line(x_2,C)`.

The examples are not selected from Euclid's Book proofs; they are among the
uniform random ZERO records.
