# DESIGN-3 E2: hindsight-only candidate policy training

Date: 2026-11-12. Label: **ZERO**. No Book proof, prefix, or goal was used
to build the training data.

## Goal-conditioned representation

`euclid_candidate_features.extract` now accepts an explicit hindsight goal;
omitting it retains the proposition goal and produces byte-identical arrays
on the rc13 fixture. The goal predicate/role vocabulary covers the E1c goal
heads, and vocabulary epoch 2 makes ordinary loading refuse epoch-1
snapshots. The model shape and feature schema did not otherwise change.

## Dataset

The tightened E1c non-trivial filter was applied, followed by the requested
removal of `pos_len`, `neq`, and direct goals. Splitting is deterministic by
`SHA256(proposition:run_seed) mod 10`: bucket zero is held out. Positions
were made by replaying only needed moves. At each position the policy target
is uniform over unapplied needed moves present in the premise-certified
candidate list (`enumerate_actions(validate=False)`). Candidates containing
reserved but not yet declared result names are omitted because they cannot
be represented as state objects. The value loss weight is zero.

## Training run and stop condition

The one approved run used a fresh width-64 model, seed `2026111202`, batch
size 16, learning rate 0.001, and a requested 2,400-second duration. It ran
inside `run-u76362.scope` with `MemoryMax=24G`, `TasksMax=1024`,
`PYTHONHASHSEED=0`, and the THREADS-1 caps.

The run stopped without operator or agent action after 1,551 seconds at
learner step 2,525. systemd recorded `Result=oom-kill`, exit 137, 24.0 GiB
peak memory, 146.1 MiB peak swap, and 3h10m36s aggregate CPU. Per the packet
rule it was **not relaunched**. The trainer writes its model only on normal
completion, so no trained snapshot exists. All processes and the scope were
gone after the failure.

| Loss receipt | Cross-entropy |
|---|---:|
| step 1 | 5.2942 |
| first 10 receipts, mean | 3.9382 |
| last 10 receipts, mean | 3.1067 |
| step 2,525 | 4.0431 |

The receipts show some aggregate reduction but are noisy; without the final
parameters they cannot support the requested transfer claim.

## Evaluation and leakage

The trained-vs-generation-0-vs-packet-8b rank tables were not run: the OOM
occurred before a trained snapshot was saved, and fabricating or substituting
an intermediate network would change the requested experiment. The smallest
operational repair is periodic atomic plain-array snapshots during training;
a new approved run would still be needed to produce the acceptance tables.

The leakage audit does not require a model. Despite the run-seed split, 45 of
the 99 distinct held-out logical-state keys also occur among the 596 training
keys. This is reported rather than silently excluded. None of the three Book
evaluation states occurs in the training positions:

| I.3 one-from-solved | 209 | 122 | no |
