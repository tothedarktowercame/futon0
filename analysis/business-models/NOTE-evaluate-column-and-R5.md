# The EVALUATE column, and three ways a criterion set fails

**Date:** 2026-08-27 · claude-13, at Joe's direction. Companion to
`NOTE-select-column-and-R14.md`; R5 is the EVALUATE ring.

Three cases, three response classes, and — the finding — **three distinct
failures of the criterion set, not three degrees of one.**

| case | response | what broke |
|---|---|---|
| **Linden Lab / Second Life** | `:declined-on-merit` | the criterion set was **empty** |
| **LangChain / Klarna** | `:engaged` | the criterion set was **incomplete and silent about it** |
| **Andela** | `:declined-ground-unknown` | the criterion was **applied but unattributed** |

## 1. Linden Lab — no dimension at all

> *"Stern, asked what the NBA island achieved, said 'I think we've had 1,200
> visitors. People tell us that's very, very good. But I can't say we have very
> precise expectations. **We just want to be there.**' **No criterion existed
> against which the observation could be scored.**"*

The observation arrived — 1,200 visitors — and had nothing to be scored against.
**This is `:warm-customer-pays` in another firm**: a signal received, with no
dimension to receive it. R5's own shape, externally witnessed.

## 2. Klarna — the sharpest case, because the criterion set was *good*

> *"Klarna reported and scaled on conversation volume, deflection rate,
> resolution time and FTE-equivalents. The CEO's own account of the reversal is
> that cost was over-weighted and quality was what suffered — **the criterion the
> firm judged the assistant by did not carry the outcome that turned out to
> matter.**"*

Four real metrics, measured, scaled, reported publicly. By ordinary standards a
good criterion set — and it failed anyway, because **it did not state what it did
not cover.** Quality was not scored, and nothing reported that quality was
unscored.

**This is R5's node statement with an external witness**: *the evaluate stage
reports what the criterion set does NOT cover, with the same discipline it
applies to a poor score.* And it is the case that **refutes the naive fix**: a
fifth metric would not have helped, because the defect was not a missing metric
but a missing statement of the boundary. Adding an entry does not make a list
self-aware.

*(Grid caveat, as with Galois in SELECT: the `:engaged` colour is about
LangChain's own sale — the record notes "nothing in the reversal is recorded as a
decision about LangChain or LangSmith". The evaluation failure is **Klarna's**,
one level out. The cell is green for the vendor and the lesson belongs to the
buyer's loop.)*

## 3. Andela — the verdict without its ground

Buyers kept buying senior engineers and stopped buying juniors. A criterion was
applied — net value of a junior placement, given the senior mentoring hours the
buyer must supply. But:

> *"the direct quotes about demand come from Andela's CEO, i.e. **the seller
> describing the buyer**; the buyer-side explanation comes from **a named
> competitor, not from a buyer**."*

So the evaluation ran, reached a verdict, and left no record attributable to the
party that held it. Hence `:declined-ground-unknown` — the class is the finding.

## The taxonomy maps onto the invariants

| case | invariant it instantiates, at criterion grain |
|---|---|
| Linden Lab | **the singularity with no dimension** — R5's own inverse-of-R14 shape |
| Klarna | **I2 typed-absence** — non-coverage not reported, so "unscored" and "scored fine" are indistinguishable |
| Andela | **I5 every-contributor-accounted** — the evaluation contributed a verdict and no attributable basis |

Three companies, three invariants, one phase. That is a stronger corroboration
than the SELECT column gave, where all four cases said the same thing.

## What it does for the excursion

**Klarna is R5's business instance**, in the way Docker is R14's — and a more
useful one, because it is the *refutation of the plausible fix*, which is the
fixture polarity hardest to source from inside the stack. A qualification record
can cite it: *this contract refuses a criterion set that does not declare its own
boundary — the failure mode that reversed a public, measured, at-scale
deployment.*

**Andela adds a requirement the excursion had not stated:** a verdict must carry
the ground held **by the party that reached it**. Second-hand attribution is
`:declined-ground-unknown` — and inside the stack it is the agent-self-report
channel problem from `E-R2`'s slice 3, where a completion summary contradicted
its own commits.
