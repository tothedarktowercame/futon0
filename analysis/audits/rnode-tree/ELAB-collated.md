- **R10** (codex; flagged by 2 agent(s)): suggest system-3-star > subsistence·being — A scheduled entrypoint and its liveness evidence monitor whether operations are occurring; they do not themselves perform the primary operation. In VSM terms this resembles intermittent audit more than System 1 work.

- **R12** (codex; flagged by 1 agent(s)): suggest system-3-star > understanding·doing — Calibration is an activity—comparing internal scores with external outcomes—and Max-Neef lists investigate, experiment, study, and analyse under understanding-doing. Understanding-being better describes dispositions such as critical conscience and receptiveness.
- **R13** (kimi-3; flagged by 1 agent(s)): suggest system-3 > protection·doing (or system-3-4) — Temporal policy depth is a SELECT-stage requirement in the catalogue: it concerns how far ahead the here-and-now action choice looks, which is Beer's control function, not S4's outside-and-future intelligence. Joe's planning turns (T277 backlog, T289 overnight guarantees) are about keeping operations on schedule, closer to control than to modelling the environment.
- **R17** (zai-4; flagged by 1 agent(s)): suggest system-5 or a system-4/5 boundary — T226/T171/T013 are re-shapings of the framework's own categories (what the ontology of missions/patterns is), which is closer to System 5 identity work than System 4 intelligence about the environment. The existing split is defensible if 'the model of self' counts as System 4 (Beer does keep the self-model in S4), so this is a soft flag.
- **R19** (codex; flagged by 1 agent(s)): suggest system-5 > identity·having, with an explicit note that catalogue-stage PERCEIVE is orthogonal — The VSM and Max-Neef placement is defensible, but the requirements catalogue labels R19 a click-time extractor in PERCEIVE. Without an orthogonality note, readers may mistake System 5 policy authorship for the computational stage that reads the policy.
- **R7** (codex; flagged by 2 agent(s)): suggest system-4 > understanding·having — Evidence-channel precision is maintained information about source reliability. Participation-being describes personal dispositions such as receptiveness and respect; it does not naturally denote a weight or reliability estimate.
- **R7** (kimi-3; flagged by 2 agent(s)): suggest system-4 > understanding·having — Evidence-channel precision is about how strongly held knowledge weights its sources; 'trust/weight/reliable' (T288, T239) are statements about the epistemic standing of information, not about participation in shared activity. - **R9** (codex; flagged by 1 agent(s)): suggest system-3-star > protection·doing — The Beer placement is apt, but Max-Neef's protection-doing cell explicitly includes taking care, preventing, cooperating, and helping. No-self-certification is an assurance safeguard; participation-interacting emphasizes affiliation and shared civic action.
