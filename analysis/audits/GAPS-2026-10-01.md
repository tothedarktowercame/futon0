## The gap rule

Inferred from the report table (shortest listed gap 6.062 h; the two ≥6 h
gaps in the current turn stream that the report does not list — 2026-08-16
21:45→2026-08-17 06:12 and 2026-09-16 00:40→12:44 — are both token- and
commit-free) and stated as the settled rule in `operator_gaps.py`:

> Consecutive qualified operator-turn timestamps delimit maximal gaps; a gap
> is listed iff it is **at least 6 hours** long and is **activity-bearing**:
> it contains ≥1 qualifying Codex `token_count` event or ≥1 commit in the
> repository scope.  Displayed bounds truncate to whole seconds.

Qualified operator rows, token dedup (last_token_usage input+output;
unchanged cumulative snapshots skipped; decreased cumulative starts a new
segment but is still counted; exact duplicates and no-info events skipped;
no cumulative counters summed), and the commit rule (unique SHA per repo,
`git log --all`, UTC committer date, mathlib4 restricted to
`DarkTower/WarMachine/`, scope = top-level `/home/joe/code/*` with a real
`.git` directory) are exactly the report's "Sources and counting limits"
rules, now executable.
